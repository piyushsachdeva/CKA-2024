## Task 25/40

In this exercise, you will learn Authentication and Authorization in Kubernetes using Service accounts

### Task details
1. Try to complete the task and steps as per the video and document your learning
2. Create a service account, give it read-only access to pods with a Role and a RoleBinding, and verify with `kubectl auth can-i`
3. Run a pod with this service account and look at the token mounted inside the pod
